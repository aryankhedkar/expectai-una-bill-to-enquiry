# 7. Approval

The product sends something in the owner's name, so the approval screen is the part of the design that matters most.

- The owner sees four things: what we read, what we will send, who it goes to, and what happens next.
- Every value is marked as read from the bill, confirmed by the owner, or corrected by the owner.
- An approval covers one version of the enquiry. A change to any value produces a new hash, voids the approval and asks again.
- The record holds who approved, when, and the hash of what they approved.
- The owner gives authority to request quotes as part of approving, which is the step a supplier would otherwise need a signed letter of authority for.
- Nothing is switched. A quote coming back is the start of a second decision, with its own approval.
