From an energy bill to an enquiry the owner approved

A small business uploads an electricity bill. The system reads it, checks what it read, asks about anything it is unsure of, drafts a quote request to suppliers, waits for approval, and sends it once.

The reasoning behind each stage is on the design page and beside the stage it belongs to. Start with a sample bill and press through the eight stages.
