# 2. Questions I would ask before building

## For the product team

After each question I have written the answer I assumed so that I could build this sketch.

1. Is an enquiry a quote request to suppliers, to a broker or to an installer? I assumed a quote request to a small panel of suppliers.
2. On whose authority is it sent? I assumed the owner gives a letter of authority when they approve.
3. How do bills arrive, whether by upload, photo, forwarded email or from accounting software? I assumed they are uploaded.
4. How many bills arrive each day, and how quickly does the owner need to see a result? I assumed hundreds a day, with a result inside a minute.
5. What does a wrong enquiry cost? I assumed it costs a wasted quote and some of the owner's trust, and that switching supplier would need a second approval.
6. Does this cover electricity only, or gas and several sites as well? I assumed one electricity meter at one site.
7. Who handles the replies? I assumed the replies come back into the product, and the sketch simulates one.
8. Which suppliers' bills are most common among current customers? I assumed nobody knows yet, which means the reader has to cope with any layout and say when it is unsure.

## For a small business owner

I would also put the following questions to a few owners.

1. Who deals with the energy bill today, and how long does it take?
2. When did you last switch supplier, and what stopped you doing it sooner?
3. What would you need to see before letting software contact a supplier in your name?
4. Which parts of the bill do you not understand?
5. What would make you stop trusting it?

I have not asked these yet, and asking them is the first thing I would do.
