A walkthrough of Bill to Enquiry

From an electricity bill to a quote request the owner has approved

We follow one sample bill through the app's own code, from the moment it is uploaded to the moment the enquiry goes out, and we stop for longest on the two places where the design matters most, which are trusting what was read and getting the owner's approval.

About five minutes, and the arrow keys work too

What I would do in the first week

Put real bills through it before automating anything

  1. 01

    Start with the common suppliers

    I would take bills from the two or three suppliers most common among current customers, because unusual layouts are where reading fails first.

  2. 02

    Check the first fifty by hand

    A person checks each extraction for the first fifty bills, and we measure accuracy per field before reducing the checking.

  3. 03

    Keep an evaluation set

    That grows into 100 to 200 real bills with the right answers written down, run on each change to the prompt or the model.
