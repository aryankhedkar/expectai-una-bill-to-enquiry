# 1. The problem

A small business owner gets an electricity bill and has no time to compare the market, so when a contract ends without a new one in place, the supply often moves onto out-of-contract rates, which are more expensive.

The aim is to take the owner from that bill to a quote request they trust enough to approve, within a few minutes, and without asking them to type anything the bill already says.

I built the design around the two ways this can go wrong, which are that the system misreads the bill or that it sends something in the owner's name that they did not agree to.
